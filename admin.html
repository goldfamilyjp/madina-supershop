Orders

Every customer order is automatically saved to the owner's Google Sheet (items, total, name, phone, fulfilment, payment, address). Open the order list with the button below.
The sheet is private to the owner's Google account (goldfamilyjp@yahoo.com) — customers and other people cannot see it.
The WhatsApp order remains the immediate alert — the sheet is the saved record for checking later.
Order numbers & customer ID: once the customer-ID feature is switched on (the Apps Script deployed and its URL pasted into the shop — see the README, “Customer ID (phone number) + order numbers”), every order is recorded in that spreadsheet's “Orders” tab and carries its own order number, MS-0001, MS-0002, … — the same number the customer sees on their ticket and in the WhatsApp message. Customers are saved under their phone number in the “Customers” tab, so their name and address fill in automatically on their next order. Until the feature is switched on, orders keep arriving in the form-responses sheet linked below, without numbers.

News board

Post a message or offer here — for example a courier-delay warning or a rice special offer. It appears at the top of the shop in an animated gold banner, and in the shop's News board under the banner photo. Write it in any language (or all three) — the shop shows each customer the matching language, falling back to the first one filled in. The newest message shows first. Coupons, vouchers & tax

🧾 Consumption tax

Applied at checkout on the product subtotal after any coupon discount. Set 0 for no tax.

Create discount codes that customers type in at checkout. A gift voucher is simply a ¥-off code with a name like GIFT1000. Customers can use one code per order; the discount comes off the product subtotal (the free-courier ¥10,000 threshold is judged before the discount, and the courier fee itself is never discounted).
