① Add product

Quick way: pick a photo — name, size and category fill in automatically. Type the price, then press Add & Save. To make a new category, type its name in the New category box.

⚡ Quick Add

This adds the product and saves it to the shop in one press. The full form below still works for stock, Japanese / Indonesian names and editing.

Stock is NOT cut automatically at order time — adjust it when an order is confirmed, so cancelled or unpaid orders never corrupt the stock count.

Tip: choose a clear photo of the packet — the product name, size and category are read from the photo and filled in automatically. Always check them; you only need to type the price yourself.

Optional second photo (e.g. the back of the packet). In the shop, customers tap the product photo to switch between the front and back photos.

After adding or changing, press "💾 Save to shop" below to publish.

② Products

Stock can also be adjusted with the −1 / +1 buttons on each product. Adjust stock when an order is confirmed — it is never cut automatically.

Tap ⭐ to put a product in the shop's top slideshow. About 10 is best.

③ Reply templates

Copy a template, fill in the bracketed blanks (customer name, total, pickup branch), and send it to the customer (WhatsApp / LINE / email). The messages themselves are in Japanese, ready to send.

④ Points

Customers earn 1% back in points (1 pt = ¥1). Points become usable 1 month after the purchase.
Add / use points only after the order and payment are confirmed on WhatsApp.
Phone numbers are stored only as hashes — the raw number is never saved.

If an order is cancelled or a mistake happens, fix the points with the adjustment above (a reason is required), or cancel an earn entry from the history below the balance.

● Points not saved yet — press the button below.

⑤ Orders

Every customer order is automatically saved to the owner's Google Sheet (items, total, name, phone, fulfilment, payment, address). Open the order list with the button below.
The sheet is private to the owner's Google account (goldfamilyjp@yahoo.com) — customers and other people cannot see it.
The WhatsApp order remains the immediate alert — the sheet is the saved record for checking later.

⑥ News board

Post a message or offer here — for example a courier-delay warning or a rice special offer. It appears at the top of the shop in an animated gold banner, and in the shop's News board under the banner photo. Write it in any language (or all three) — the shop shows each customer the matching language, falling back to the first one filled in. The newest message shows first. Press "💾 Save to shop" to publish.
